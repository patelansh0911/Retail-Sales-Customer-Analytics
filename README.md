# Retail Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a synthetic retail dataset of 50,000 orders to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, discounts, profitability, returns, and payment channels.

The project uses Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI to transform raw sales data into interactive dashboards and actionable business insights.

## Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, KPIs, DAX measures, and slicers
* **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, and exploratory data analysis
* **SQL Server:** Data querying, aggregation, and analysis
* **Excel:** Data validation and reporting

## Dataset

* 50,000 order records
* 5,000 unique customers
* 20 unique products
* 86,085 units sold

The dataset is synthetic and intended for portfolio and learning purposes.

## Key Performance Indicators

## Key Findings

* Electronics is the leading revenue category, generating approximately ₹488.23 million.
* Laptops generate approximately ₹248.20 million in revenue.
* The 0% discount band has a profit margin of approximately 17.47%, compared with 14.17% for the 16%+ discount band.
* Approximately 5.92% of orders are marked as returned.
* Revenue was approximately ₹499.96 million in 2024 and ₹499.09 million in 2025.

## Business Recommendations

* Monitor product-level profitability alongside revenue.
* Review high-discount transactions and their impact on margins.
* Investigate return patterns by product and category.
* Analyze repeat purchases to identify customer-retention opportunities.
* Compare monthly performance to identify trends and investigate changes.
